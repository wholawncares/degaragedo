Warning signs

Signs You Need Garage Door Repair in Wawaset Park

01

A door that moves slowly is often struggling with worn rollers, a failing spring, or an opener that is losing power. Ignoring it can lead to a complete failure. We diagnose the cause and fix it before it leaves you stranded.

02

Grinding usually means metal-on-metal contact from worn bearings or rollers. Squealing often points to dry hinges or a failing opener gear. Both are fixable, and addressing them early prevents damage to the door and opener.

03

An uneven door is a sign of a broken spring, a snapped cable, or a track that is out of alignment. This is a safety issue because the door can fall. Stop using the door and call us for same-day service.

04

Intermittent remote issues often mean a weak battery, a failing logic board, or interference. If your keypad works but the remote does not, we can program a new remote or repair the opener. We test everything before we leave.

05

A door that reverses for no reason usually has misaligned sensors or a sensitivity setting that is too high. This is a safety feature, but it makes the door unusable. We realign the sensors and adjust the opener to UL 325 standards.
