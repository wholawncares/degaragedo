Warning signs

Signs You Need Garage Door Repair in East Side

01

Grinding, squealing, or popping sounds usually mean worn rollers, dry hinges, or a failing bearing. In East Side, rust from humidity makes this worse. Lubrication can help, but if the noise persists, a part is likely failing. We can replace the worn components before they cause a bigger breakdown.

02

If one side of the door lifts before the other, you likely have a broken spring or a stretched cable. This puts stress on the opener and the tracks. It is not safe to keep running the door this way. We can replace the spring or cable and get it level again.

03

This usually means the opener is working but the drive system has failed. It could be a stripped gear, a broken chain or belt, or a disconnected trolley. We can diagnose the opener and repair or replace the failed part. Sometimes a simple repair saves you the cost of a new opener.

04

This is almost always a sensor problem. The safety sensors are misaligned or dirty, so the opener thinks something is in the way. We can realign the sensors and check the wiring. If the sensors are bad, we can replace them.

05

A worn bottom seal or weather stripping lets in water, drafts, and pests. In East Side, the humidity and rain can rust your tracks and hinges if water gets in. We can replace the seal and weather stripping to keep your garage dry.
