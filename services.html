Garage Door Repair

Our core service covers whatever is keeping your door from running smooth: binding tracks, worn rollers, a dead motor, or a door that slams down. We diagnose on site, quote the fix, and finish most jobs the same day across Wilmington.

Garage Door Installation

New door going in? We set the tracks level, tension the springs to the door weight, and dial in the opener travel so it runs quiet from day one. Steel, wood, and insulated doors installed for Wilmington homes and small commercial bays.

Garage Door Opener Repair

A humming opener that will not lift usually means a stripped gear or a bad capacitor, not a whole new unit. We repair LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, and Marantec openers and only replace what failed.

View service 04

Garage Door Opener Installation

We install belt-drive and chain-drive openers with the safety eyes wired to UL 325 spec. If your ceiling is low or the header is tight, we will tell you which unit fits before we cut anything.

View service 05

Garage Door Spring Repair

A broken torsion spring is the number one call we get. We match the wire gauge and cycle rating to your door, wind it with proper bars, and test the balance so the opener is not doing the lifting.

View service 06

Garage Door Cable Repair

Snapped cables whip and can drop a door fast. We replace both galvanized steel cables as a pair, check the drums for wear, and reset the tension so it does not happen again next month.

View service 07

Garage Door Panel Replacement

One dented or rusted section does not mean a whole new door. We match panel profiles and colors from Clopay, Amarr, and Wayne Dalton and swap the damaged section so the door looks factory again.

View service 08

Garage Door Roller Repair

Noisy, grinding doors are usually nylon rollers that have flat-spotted or steel rollers that lost their bearings. We swap them out, lube the hinges, and quiet the door down on the same visit.

View service 09

Garage Door Hinge Repair

Frozen or bent hinges make a door shimmy and bind. We replace the worn hinges, check the track brackets, and make sure each section rolls straight instead of fighting the one above it.

View service 10

Garage Door Track Repair

A door that jumped the track after a bump needs the track straightened or replaced, not hammered back. We realign the vertical and horizontal track, re-bolt the brackets, and verify the door runs true.

View service 11

Garage Door Sensor Repair

If the door reverses for no reason, the safety eyes are misaligned, dirty, or the wiring is loose. We realign the sensors, test the beam, and confirm the reverse works the way UL 325 requires.

View service 12

Garage Door Remote Programming & Repair

Lost a remote or bought a used one that will not sync? We program LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Linear remotes on site and replace the battery or logic board if the remote itself is dead.

View service 13

Garage Door Keypad Repair

A keypad that beeps but will not open is usually a dead battery or a code that got wiped. We reprogram it, replace the unit if the buttons are shot, and walk you through setting a new PIN.

Garage Door Maintenance & Tune-Up

Once a year we lube the rollers and hinges, check the spring tension, tighten the track bolts, and test the safety reverse. It is the cheapest way to keep a door from failing in January.

Garage Door Weather Stripping & Sealing

A gap at the bottom seal lets in cold air, water, and mice. We replace the bottom seal and the perimeter weather stripping so your garage stays dry and your energy bill stops climbing.

Garage Door Off-Track Repair

A door off track is dangerous and should not be forced. We secure it, inspect the rollers and brackets, reset the door on the track, and test the full cycle before we leave.

Garage Door Dent Removal

Garage Door Chain & Belt Replacement

A stretched chain slaps and a frayed belt snaps. We replace the chain or belt, set the tension, and adjust the opener limits so the door stops at the right spot every time.

View service 27

Garage Door Gear & Sprocket Repair

Stripped gears are why your opener hums but nothing moves. We pull the motor cover, replace the gear and sprocket kit, and re-grease the drive so it runs quiet again.

View service 28

Garage Door Bearing & Bushing Repair

Worn center bearings and end bushings make a door groan and drag. We replace them, re-tension the springs, and get the torsion tube spinning free instead of grinding.

View service 29

Garage Door Drum Replacement

A cracked or grooved cable drum will chew through cables. We replace the drums, rewind the cables evenly, and reset the spring tension so the door lifts level.

Garage Door Bracket Repair
