01

Usually a broken torsion spring or a cable that has slipped off the drum. The opener is trying to lift a door that has lost its counterbalance. Stop using the door and call us, because running it can burn out the opener motor.

02

That sound is almost always a torsion spring snapping. It happens most often in cold weather. Do not try to open the door. The spring is under extreme tension and needs a trained tech with winding bars to replace it safely.

03

Sensor misalignment is the usual cause. One sensor may have been bumped or the lenses may be dirty. This is a quick fix, often under 45 minutes, but it will keep the door from closing until it is corrected.

04
