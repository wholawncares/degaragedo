Warning signs

Signs You Need Garage Door Repair in Forty Acres

01

That bang is almost always a broken torsion spring. The door becomes too heavy for the opener to lift, and forcing it can strip the opener gear or snap a cable. Stop using the opener and call for repair. This is the most common emergency we run in Forty Acres.

02

Usually a misaligned or dirty safety sensor, or a travel limit that has drifted. It is a safety feature working as designed, but it makes the door unusable. We realign the sensors and reset the limits, and we test the auto-reverse before we leave.

03

Worn steel rollers or dry hinges are the usual cause. Left alone, the grinding wears the track and puts strain on the opener. A roller and hinge replacement is inexpensive and quiets the door immediately. We use nylon rollers on attached garages.

04

A snapped cable or a worn cable drum lets one side drop. Driving the opener can pull the door off track and bend the panels. This is a stop-using-it situation. We replace both cables and check the drums and springs at the same time.

05

A stripped gear or a dead motor. On older Genie and Chamberlain units, the gear is often the cheap failure point, not the motor. We check the springs first because weak springs overload the opener, then repair the gear or replace the motor.
