Warning signs

Signs You Need Garage Door Repair in Southbridge

01

A door that moves slower than usual often indicates a worn spring or a failing opener motor. In Southbridge, the humidity can cause springs to lose tension over time. If you notice the door lagging, it is best to have it checked before it stops working entirely.

02

Grinding noises usually mean the rollers or hinges are worn and need lubrication or replacement. Squealing can indicate a problem with the opener's gears or chain. In older Southbridge homes, we often find that the original rollers have never been replaced.

03

If the door is crooked or has come off the track, do not try to force it. This is a safety hazard and can cause serious injury. Call us immediately, and we will secure the door and repair the track. This is a common issue after a vehicle accidentally backs into the door.

04

If your remote stops working, it could be a dead battery, a programming issue, or a faulty receiver board. We can diagnose and fix the problem quickly. For Southbridge customers, we often find that the keypad has corroded from river humidity.

05

A door that reverses for no reason is usually a sign of misaligned sensors or a problem with the safety reverse system. This is a safety feature, but it can be frustrating. We can realign the sensors and test the system to ensure it meets UL 325 safety standard.
