Warning signs

Signs You Need Garage Door Repair in North Brandywine

01

That bang is almost always a torsion spring snapping. The door will feel extremely heavy or will not open at all. Stop using the opener immediately, because running it with a broken spring can burn out the motor or drop the door. Call for same-day service.

02

A door that is uneven usually has a snapped or stretched cable, a worn drum, or a broken spring on one side. Forcing it will damage the track and panels. This is a safety issue and should be looked at the same day.

03

If you hear the motor but the door stays put, the likely cause is a stripped gear, a broken chain or belt, or a disconnected trolley. On older LiftMaster and Chamberlain units, a gear kit is often the fix rather than a whole new opener.

04

This is usually a sensor problem. The safety sensors at the bottom of the track are misaligned, dirty or have a broken wire. It is a quick fix, but do not bypass the sensors, because they are required under UL 325 and they protect kids and pets.

05

Noise means worn rollers, dry hinges or a track that is out of alignment. Left alone, the vibration loosens brackets and wears the springs faster. A tune-up with new rollers and lubrication usually solves it and adds years to the door.
